Depth comes from the colour step first — paper to surface to haze — and from the hairline. Shadows are the exception: --mk-shadow-card for a card that must float over content, --mk-shadow-lift for a dialog. They are low-opacity night, never tinted blue.

Glows are retired. Nothing in this identity emits light: no coloured halo, no blurred ring, no filter. A focus ring is a solid 3px dusk ring with no blur, and a hover is a 6% tint.
