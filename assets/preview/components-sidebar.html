Sidebar item

Items read as ink on paper at 15.5:1 — never muted, because a navigation label is a target, not metadata. Hover fills with a 6% dusk tint. The active item takes dusk text, a 12% tint and a 2px dusk bar at the left edge.

Group headers are the one muted element here: JetBrains Mono 11px, uppercase, --mk-fg-2. Radius is 4px on items, 8px on the panel.
