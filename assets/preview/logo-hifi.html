One mark, built from two shapes you already read as a command line: the chevron you type after, and the block cursor that answers — given two ears. Flat colour, no gradient, no glow.

Construction

20-unit grid · 240 × 170

Both shapes sit on the same grid. The chevron is a 16-unit stroke bent at 90°; the cursor is a caret block 120 units wide with two 38-unit ears cut from its top edge. Nothing is rounded and nothing is outlined.

Rules

do · don't

Do

  • Keep one cursor width of clear space on all four sides.
  • Pick the cut that matches the ground: paper, night, one colour.
  • Drop to meow-symbol-small.svg under 24px and to the favicon at 16px.
  • Let the mark carry the only accent on the surface.

Don't

  • No gradient fill, no glow, no drop shadow, no outline.
  • No rotating, stretching, re-colouring or re-spacing the parts.
  • No re-typing the wordmark in a live font.
  • No lockup under 96px wide, and no second animation beside the blink.
