01 · Anatomy

symbol · 228u gap 62u wordmark · meowkit

The gap between the cursor and the first letter is 62 units — about half the cursor's width. It is the only adjustable measure in the file, and it is already set; do not re-space it.

02 · Clear space and minimums

Clear space
Keep one cursor width — 120 units, about 13.7% of the lockup's width — free on all four sides. Nothing crosses it: no text, no rule, no card edge.
